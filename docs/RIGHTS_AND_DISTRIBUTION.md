# Rights and distribution notes

The original PC developer has objected to the previous project branding, redistribution of its assets and songs, and reverse engineering under its EULA. The old distributions are withdrawn. No developer approval, asset licence or legal clearance is claimed for this project.

v1.5.0 removes original PC artwork, fonts, sounds and songs from the distributed application and corresponding source. Protected bundled-song extraction/decryption tooling and keys are excluded. User-supplied resource installation is a technical separation, not a legal exemption or a grant of rights.

17 U.S.C. §1201 addresses unauthorized circumvention of effective access controls and certain circumvention technology. Its interoperability provisions have specific conditions; they are not a blanket authorization for a port, decompilation, copyrighted asset reuse or song decryption. Section 1201(c) separately preserves other copyright questions. Contractual restrictions in the original EULA are an additional issue. A software/IP lawyer should assess any claimed permission or exception before relying on it.

The desktop importer reads only fingerprinted plain serialized asset files and explicit interface-audio files. It has no encryption keys, protected-song support, DRM bypass or executable/decompiler input. It refuses unsupported asset fingerprints. These are technical findings, not a determination that extracting or using those files is authorized in every case.

Do not distribute original PC resources, imported asset packs, original executables, decrypted songs, keys, user data or proprietary SDK/firmware. Use only material you are authorized to use. Keep third-party notices intact. Independently licensed alternatives must be checked component by component, including their artwork, fonts and linked dependencies.
